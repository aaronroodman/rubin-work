# MTAOS step for 20261108 seq 468

> **Status:** current · **Last updated:** 2026-11-10 · **Kind:** reference (visit report)

The large Tweak in effect during seq 468 came from the Optical Feedback Control (OFC)
step computed from seq 466. Seq 466 had wavefront measurements on only two of the four
corner wavefront sensors (CWFS), but the Main Telescope Active Optics System (MTAOS) did
not skip it. OFC solved all 22 degrees of freedom (DOF) from those two corners. The
proportional-only PID controller then applied 0.3 of that state, and the clip did not
limit it, because the clip acts only on the integral term.

Notebook: `notebooks/mtaos_visit_step_v1.ipynb`. Run output:
`output/20261109_visit_20261108_00468/` (PDF, summary and 22-DOF CSVs). Code:
`code/byvisit.py`. Sources are the Engineering Facility Database (EFD) and the
Consolidated Database (ConsDB). The reproduction uses the summit's ts_ofc v4.8.1 and the
v13 MTAOS configuration from ts_config_mttcs v0.22.12.

## Numbers

step's EFD `visitDoF`. "If P clipped" is the Tweak that would result if the
`max_integral` limits applied to the P term. "Trim after" is the aggregated DOF after
the step. The v-modes are the 12 controlled singular modes of the normalized
sensitivity matrix.

## Open items

- Confirm the skip mechanism in the MTAOS code that writes the "4/4 wavefront error
  measurements" line. The CSC version was v0.30.0-alpha.5.
- Seq 468's corners 191 and 195 read Z4 = -7.99 and -9.22 um, while 199 and 203 read
  under 1 um. The camera tilt from the seq 466 step (rx +0.0199 deg, ry -0.0609 deg) may
  have caused this; untested.
- The ConsDB `ccdvisit1_quicklook` z4-z28 differ from the EFD deviation. It is not
  settled whether ConsDB holds the OPD or uses another frame.
- The v-modes come from the summit's ts_ofc and normalization, not from
  `rubinwork.aos_state`. The local stack differs from the summit by 4-28% relative.
