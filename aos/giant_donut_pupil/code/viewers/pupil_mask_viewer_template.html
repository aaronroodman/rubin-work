What changed, in numbers

Only the silhouette changesM1, M1Baffle1, M2, M3, the filter, L3 and the spider are identical in all six configuration files.
v4.02gOne circle per field angle fitted to the whole traced silhouette outline, then a cubic over 0–2 deg. The outline is 165 polygons at z 3.48–5.12 m, not a circle, so the one arc that matters sat 1.8–5.3 pixels inside Batoid at 1.45–1.75 deg.
v4.02sBelow 1.4 deg, the v4.02g circle. From 1.4 deg, a second circle fitted to Batoid's inner edge only where the silhouette sets it: center 130–200 mm further off-axis, radius 70–130 mm smaller, outermost point 52–69 mm (4.3–5.6 pixels) further into the ring.
CaveatsA 4.3–4.6 pixel jump at 1.40 deg, so donuts at 1.3–1.4 deg still use the old circle. The second circle is fitted only to 1.75 deg but runs to 2.0 deg.
