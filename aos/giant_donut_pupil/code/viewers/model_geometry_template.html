What the camera silhouette is

LSST_r_v4.02 replaces v1000's single CameraBody disk with CameraSilhouette: 165 flat polygon slices, one every 10 mm in height from z 3.481 to 5.121 m. Each slice is the outer boundary of all camera parts at its height, taken from the camera CAD; their radii are 796–825 mm. v1000's CameraBody is one disk of radius 0.80469 m (the camera's 1.609 m outer diameter) at z 3.500 m, attached to the camera.

Both are items between M2 and M3 in the trace, so they catch light on its way down from M2 to M3 as it passes the camera. That beam is an annulus converging on M3; at large field it shifts sideways, and its inner part runs into the camera body. The rays at 1.7 deg show it: stop-plane x −2.95 and −2.90 m are stopped by slices at z 4.05 and 4.46 m in v4.02, and by CameraBody in v1000; the next rays in are stopped by M2's central hole.

The camera body in danish's obscuration files

danish v1000 and v3.14 (RubinObsc_v1000_r_rtpp0_azp45_pp0d0.yaml, …v3.14…) both carry a CameraBody circle: radius 1.960 m in the stop plane, its center moving 0.589 m per deg of field, fitted at the nominal configuration. Batoid v3.14 itself has no camera body. The study's masks call it CameraSilhouette. Outermost point along −x in the stop plane:
