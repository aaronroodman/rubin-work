In the corner-WFS wavefront comparison (Danish unpaired mode, block-T668 / 20260713 TARTS test), Z11 differs markedly between the intra-focal and extra-focal sides (intra ≈ +0.15 µm, extra ≈ −0.10 µm), and Z14 shows the same intra/extra behavior. Individual paired and unpaired results are quite stable; paired-mode Z11 is negative and jumps around more.

**Why:** This split is an unexplained mystery, NOT an expected/known effect. Do not describe it as "known" or "expected."

**How to apply:** When discussing intra/extra focal differences in CWFS Zernikes, treat the Z11/Z14 split as an open question. Related analysis in [[danish-tarts-compare-notebook]].
